About Mindful

A private, lightweight meditation companion. Your session controls run on this device. It is not a medical treatment or a substitute for professional support.

Install on iPhone

Open this app in Safari, tap Share, then choose “Add to Home Screen”. Once installed and first opened online, the core app can work offline.

Three simple practices

Mindful pause · 1 minute
Notice three things you can feel, two things you can hear, and one thing you can see.

Body scan · 3 minutes
Move attention from your feet upward and notice sensation without needing to change anything.

Compassion breath · 2 minutes
Breathe in warmth; breathe out softness toward yourself and others.
